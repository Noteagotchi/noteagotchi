# Personal Knowledge Graphs (PKG)

> A personal knowledge graph (PKG) is a new class of software that allows you to efficiently take notes in the form of flexible non-linear knowledge graphs. PKGs have evolved from older linear note-taking and outlining software.

More academically, a PKG is:

> ...a resource of structured information about entities personally related to its user, their attributes and the relations between them.

Essentially, the modern evolution of PKGs, is a combination of hyperlinks, mardown and graphs to organize information.

## PKG Advantages

1. quickly organize thoughts
2. lowers your cognitive load
3. promotes non-linear thinking
4. long-term persistence
5. breaks writers' block
